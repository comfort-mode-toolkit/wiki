Research at Comfort Commons isn't about writing papers for journals. It's about understanding real human needs so we can build tools that actually help.

What We Research
================

We investigate questions like:
* "Why do certain high-contrast themes cause nausea?"
* "What stops developers from using accessibility tools?"
* "How do different cultures perceive color meaning?"

How to Contribute
=================

1. **Read our Ethics First:** Before you ask a single question, read our :doc:`ethics` guide. It's non-negotiable.
2. **Find a Topic:** Check our `Research Issues <https://github.com/comfort-mode-toolkit/cm-hub/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22research%22>`_.
3. **Share Findings:** Comment on issues with what you find. Links, summaries, and personal experiences are all valid data.

Methodologies
=============

We use diverse methods:
* **Literature Review:** Reading existing papers and articles.
* **Community Listening:** Learning from public discussions (see :doc:`social-media`).
* **User Interviews:** Talking to people directly (requires specific approval).
* **Lived Experience:** Sharing your own story.

----

*Research without respect is just extraction. We prioritize people over data.*
