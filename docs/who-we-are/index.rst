Our Values
==========

We believe in:
* **Autonomy:** People should control their own tools.
* **Dignity:** Technology should respect human needs.
* **Community:** We build better when we build together.

The Team

We are a loose collective of:
* **Maintainers:** The gardeners who keep the weeds out.
* **Contributors:** The builders who add new rooms.
* **Researchers:** The explorers who find new paths.
* **Users:** The reason we do all of this.
