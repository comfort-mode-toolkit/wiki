================
Research Ethics
================

*Protecting everyone who makes this work possible*

----

Our Sacred Law
==============

**People first, research second.** Always and forever.

We're building tools to help people thrive, not studying them like specimens. Every person who shares their story deserves respect, privacy, and dignity.

The Unbreakable Rules
=====================

1. No Labels, Ever
------------------
People are not categories.
* **Do:** "people who experience vestibular challenges"
* **Don't:** "vestibular patients" or "disabled users"

2. Consent is Sacred
--------------------
* Always ask before quoting someone.
* Get clear permission before sharing screenshots.
* Honor "no" immediately and without question.

3. Privacy by Design
--------------------
* Anonymize everything unless credit is requested.
* Scrub usernames and identifying details.
* Store personal data securely and delete it when done.

4. Community Respect
--------------------
* We are guests in other communities.
* Don't treat communities as "subjects."
* Give back findings and resources; don't just extract data.

Red Flags
=========

**Stop your research immediately if:**
* Someone is in distress.
* You are working with minors without strict protections.
* You are venturing into medical territory (we are builders, not doctors).
* A community expresses discomfort with your presence.

**When in doubt, pause and ask.**

----

*Good ethics isn't just following rules—it's about building trust.*
