Community contribution is about people. It's about making sure everyone feels welcome, heard, and supported.

Ways to Help
============

**Welcome Newcomers**
   Keep an eye on "Good First Issues" and say hello to new contributors. Offer help if they get stuck.

**Triage Issues**
   Help organize bug reports. Can you reproduce the bug? Is the description clear?

**Facilitate Discussions**
   Keep conversations constructive. Ask clarifying questions.

**Spread the Word**
   Share our tools with people who need them.

Moderation
==========

We take our :doc:`culture` seriously. If you see behavior that violates our values, report it to the maintainers.
