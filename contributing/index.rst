==================
Contributor Guides
==================

*Choose your own adventure*

----

Welcome to the heart of Comfort Commons. This is where you find out how to help. We've organized everything by what you want to *do*, not just who you *are*.

Pick a path below to get started.

.. grid:: 2

    .. grid-item-card::  :octicon:`code` Write Code
        :link: code/index
        :link-type: doc

        Build tools, fix bugs, and improve our infrastructure.
        
        **Best for:** Developers, engineers, tinkerers.

    .. grid-item-card::  :octicon:`beaker` Research
        :link: research/index
        :link-type: doc

        Investigate accessibility challenges and gather community wisdom.
        
        **Best for:** Researchers, academics, curious people.

    .. grid-item-card::  :octicon:`book` Write Docs
        :link: documentation/index
        :link-type: doc

        Make our tools easier to use and understand.
        
        **Best for:** Writers, teachers, clear thinkers.

    .. grid-item-card::  :octicon:`heart` Build Community
        :link: community/index
        :link-type: doc

        Welcome newcomers, moderate discussions, and keep the culture healthy.
        
        **Best for:** Community managers, empathetic leaders.

    .. grid-item-card::  :octicon:`check-circle` Test & Verify
        :link: testing/index
        :link-type: doc

        Ensure our tools actually work for real people.
        
        **Best for:** QA testers, users with diverse needs.

----

Not sure where to start?
========================

If you're overwhelmed, just head to our :doc:`/getting-started` page for a gentle introduction. You can also jump into our `Discussions <https://github.com/comfort-mode-toolkit/cm-hub/discussions>`_ and say hello. We'll help you find your place.
