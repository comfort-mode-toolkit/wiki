================
Our Culture
================

*How we treat each other in the seaside forest*

----

We Play
=======
We build sandcastles, doodle wild ideas, and experiment without fear. Play is how we learn. If you have a wild idea, try it! If it flops, we'll laugh and learn together.

We Are Kind
===========
Kindness is our baseline. We respect boundaries, celebrate wins, and make space for imperfect people. Because we are all imperfect.

We Listen
=========
Everyone has something to teach. We listen to voices that don't usually get heard. If someone says "this isn't working for me," that's a gift, not a complaint.

We Welcome Everyone
===================
You don't need credentials to be here. You don't need to be a "coder." Curiosity and kindness are the only requirements.

We Build for Real Needs
=======================
We're not here to impress the industry. We're here to make tech softer and more humane for real people.

We Are Open
===========
Our work, our mistakes, our messy drafts—they're all open. Lurking is fine. Asking questions is better.

We Grow Together
================
Every sandcastle is a team effort. If you teach, you're a leader. If you ask for help, you're brave.

----

*Come as you are. Build, play, learn.*
